# Teacher Guide: Beginner Coach Score Course

## Teaching Principle

The course follows this cycle:

```text
understand → observe → define → sketch → test → implement → review
```

AI-assisted coding appears only after students identify and test a human need.

## Suggested Sessions

| Session | Lessons | Main artifact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1–2 | Computer vocabulary and terminal checkpoint |
| 2 | 3 | Verified Flutter environment |
| 3 | 4–5 | Running Hello World and Coach Score |
| 4 | 6 | Everyday-app HCI map |
| 5 | 7 | Observation sheet and problem statement |
| 6 | 8 | Tested paper prototype |
| 7 | 9 | Safe AI explanation and plan |
| 8–9 | 10 | Reviewed small change and contribution note |

## Facilitation Rules

- Demonstrate once, then let students perform the task.
- Use fictional rosters, messages, photos, and match data.
- Do not require social-media accounts.
- During usability tests, do not teach the interface while measuring it.
- Evaluate observation quality and reasoning, not artistic drawing skill.
- Keep Copilot command approvals enabled and review diffs before commits.
- Provide a non-AI path for students who cannot use an AI account.

## Evidence of Learning

Collect one artifact from each stage:

1. Vocabulary checkpoint
2. Terminal navigation demonstration
3. `flutter doctor -v` checkpoint
4. Running Hello World
5. Coach Score workflow notes
6. HCI concept map
7. Usability observation sheet
8. Paper prototype and test notes
9. Safe AI plan
10. Contribution note, diff review, and verification results
